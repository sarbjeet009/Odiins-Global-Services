Eligibility Guidelines

WHO CAN APPLY?

Candidates must meet basic regulatory guidelines established by the bank's Business Correspondent framework.

🎓

12th Pass or Above

Must have completed at least Intermediate (+2) in any stream. Basic computer and numerical literacy required.

🎂

Age 21+ & Indian Citizen

Applicant must be at least 21 years of age, holding a valid Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and permanent residence in Odisha.

💳

No Loan Default

Clean CIBIL track record. The applicant or family member must not have defaulted on any bank loans.

⚖️

No Criminal Record

Clean police background check. Clear background verification is mandatory for financial custody operations.

🏪

Own / Rented Shop Space

A physical commercial shop, counter, or office space (min. 100 sq ft) with electrical power connection.

📍

Within 15 KM of Link Branch

Your designated shop location must be within a 15 km radius of the parent public sector bank link branch.
