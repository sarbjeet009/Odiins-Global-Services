Hiring in Bhubaneswar: Top Skills Small Businesses are Looking for in 2026

By Odiins Research Desk • March 15, 2026 • 4 min read

Bhubaneswar’s commercial landscape is transforming rapidly. Beyond the IT corridors of Infocity and Chandaka, thousands of small and medium businesses—retail showrooms, wholesale traders in Unit 1 and Mancheswar, diagnostic labs, logistics hubs, and restaurants—are scaling up operations.

Yet, business owners frequently report the same hurdle: finding dependable manpower that combines basic technical readiness with long-term commitment. In this report, our placement team outlines the top four competencies local employers prioritize today.

1. Practical Bilingual Communication (Odia + Hindi/English)

Whether staffing a retail showroom in Saheed Nagar or running telecaller operations, fluency in conversational Odia combined with functional Hindi or English has become non-negotiable. Customers expect warmth, local relatability, and polite telephone etiquette.

2. Working Knowledge of Billing & Computer Software

The era of manual ledger records is firmly behind us. Small grocery distributors, apparel stores, and hardware retailers in Cuttack and Bhubaneswar look for back-office assistants comfortable with Tally, Vyapar, basic Excel, and digital UPI point-of-sale machines.

3. Punctuality and Local Proximity

High attrition frequently stems from unmanageable commutes. Businesses prefer candidates residing within a 5–8 km radius who can reliably reach work on time without reliance on erratic long-distance inter-city buses.

4. Professional Integrity and Verification

With cash handling and customer data involved, business owners prioritize candidates with verifiable identity documents, local references, and a clear record.

“A degree gets you an interview, but reliability, honesty, and local commitment get you hired and promoted in Odisha.”
