Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Odiins in Odisha.

1. Do job seekers have to pay any registration fee?

▼

No, registering as a job seeker on Odiins is completely free. We do not charge candidates any consultation or placement fees. Our mission is to connect skilled Odias with genuine opportunities without financial hurdles.

2. How quickly can a business hire staff through Odiins?

▼

Once you submit your requirement, our local team reviews our active candidate database and reaches back within 24 hours with matching, pre-screened candidate profiles ready for telephonic or in-person interviews.

3. How do you verify household help and drivers?

▼

We check government identity documents (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving License), verify local addresses, check previous work references, and facilitate personal interviews so you have peace of mind inviting help to your home.

4. In which districts does Odiins operate?

▼

Odiins covers all 30 districts of Odisha. While our primary hubs are in Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Puri, Rourkela, Sambalpur, and Berhampur, we assist businesses and candidates across every district of our state.

5. Can I book a pandit for family pujas and Griha Pravesh?

▼

Yes! We connect you with experienced, Vedic-trained Odia Brahmins and pandits for Satyanarayan Puja, Griha Pravesh, Rudrabhishek, marriage rituals, and festive pujas across Odisha cities.
